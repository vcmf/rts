# 0004 The first river type is the lowland meander

## Context

Water was the weakest part of the prototype and one river type had to be designed fully first.

## Decision

We will build the lowland meander first (the Goldbach at Austerlitz): bends that turn back, 4–11 m of water, pools and riffles, a flat floodplain. Nothing stands in the water except stones.

## Consequences

Other river types (stony brook, wide slow river, braided gravel) come later on the same system.

## Alternatives considered

Starting with a generic river shader.
