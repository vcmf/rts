# 0009 A battle takes 30–45 minutes of real time

## Context

Real battles lasted hours; play sessions cannot.

## Decision

We will tune march speeds and casualty rates so a battle takes 30–45 minutes, with speed controls (pause, 1×, 2×, 4–8×). Time of day is chosen per scenario and baked; the sun does not move during a battle.

## Consequences

Calibration targets time-to-break, not historical hit rates.

## Alternatives considered

Real-time battles; a moving sun.

See: `docs/proposals/sim-design.md` §2, §8.
