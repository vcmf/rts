# 0031 Trees are generated from species data

## Context

Every tree on the field is a pack model (`src/look/nature.ts`), and the art bible asks for species the pack does
not have (oak, lime, birch, willow, alder, orchards, conifers, hedgerows: `docs/art-bible.md` §4b, §6c, §11). L7 still
owes bare winter trees (`docs/plans/active/track-e-look.md` L7). The research
(`docs/research/procedural-trees.md`) found standard methods for growing trees, and a prototype grew all of these
species from data (`prototypes/tree-gen.html`, plan `docs/plans/active/tree-generator.md`). On the look page
(`?trees=gen,cards`) the generated trees take the whole painted look. Because the generator knows each tree's
clumps and branches, the cards can light each clump as its own volume, and the crowns can thin to bare branches by
season. The owner (2026-10-14): the prototype is "absolutely stunning"; this track exists to improve the trees.
On the map engine's branch, decision 0029 keeps free-standing trees as a list the look places "with its form,
height and crown": the generator is where that form comes from.

Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-14 for its look ("better, more beautiful") and on the expectation of no
performance regression. The default arm is measured unchanged (identical goldens); the generated arm's frame cost is
not measured yet, and the plan's cost bench (`docs/plans/active/tree-generator.md`) confirms or revisits it.

## Decision

We will generate the field's trees offline from species data, and stop taking them from packs:
- **species as data:** a crown envelope, a height range and a few growth numbers per species (era content, decision
  0015); a seed per variant, so a model is the same every time;
- **space colonization** grows the branches inside the envelope, the pipe model sizes them, and the branch tips
  are clustered into the art bible's 3–6 crown clumps (§5);
- **the crown is leaf cards** (L7a), and each card is lit in the **ellipsoid of its own clump**, not the crown's:
  every clump has its own lit top, dark core and light through its edge;
- **deciduous crowns lose their leaves with the season**: from late autumn the cards drop one by one and shrink,
  a few tufts left clinging, the twigs appear, and the shadows are baked with the crown the map's season has.
  Conifers stay green (§4b).

## Consequences

- Good: every species and variant the maps need, and the bare winter trees L7 owes, from one generator.
- Good: Tiny Glade's look of a crown as several lit puffs, from data the generator already has.
- Good: the shadows follow the crowns: a bare tree casts a bare tree's shadow.
- Bad: winter crowns cost more triangles (the twigs: about 3–4k per broadleaf, measured in the prototype); not yet
  measured on the page or the min-spec laptop.
- Bad: twigs thinner than a shadow texel (0.33 m) cast no shadow: a bare wood's shadow is its limbs only.
- Bad: the generator's output must keep within the placement rule; on the look page each model is sized to the pack
  class it replaces.

## Alternatives considered

- **Pack models:** too few species, no bare winter variants, and a shape language the art bible rejected (§5).
- **Generated by Tripo from prompts** (§10): one model per prompt, no seeds or variants, no branch structure for
  winter, no clump data for the light.
- **SpeedTree or The Grove:** realistic leaf crowns, offline tools outside our pipeline; our crowns are clumps.
