Reference for the map view (decision 0058, which governs where they differ; its canopy and field strips were tried
and rejected): a 2D map with a 3D feel. Hill shading under one light (ridges
and valleys read at a glance), woods as massed dark-green canopy texture, villages and farms as small red-roofed
clusters, roads and field strips drawn thin, rivers in pale blue. Armies as small coloured blocks with a slight
raised edge and drop shadow, flags and portraits on staffs as labels, place names in small caps serif. Smoke,
fire and a highlight ring as the only effects.
